## About:

Hello future employers, fans, and subjects. Punch Up!! is a spiritual successor to Nintendo's Punch Out series. I initially started it as a project in 2023 since I wanted to me a game and the idea seemed simple. I finished it that year but it was in a barely playable state ([Original Project](https://github.com/TheRussT/Punch-Up---)). Armed with more experience, I decided to make an updated version which is how we get here. This project is entirely my own no AI was used for coding or artwork.

## How To Play:

Simply go to releases and download Punch_Up_New.exe. Currently there is only one release for windows, but I hope to release for more platforms in the future!

## Gameplay:

The gameplay revolves around a few keys in a manner similar to an NES controller

- A/D Key: Left/Right Dodge. Can be used to avoid enemy attacks. Duration can be 
influenced by holding key or pressing opposite dodge.

- S Key: Duck. Dodges majority of attacks but has more lag as a result.

- W Key: Parry/Block. Raises player's guard too block certain weak attacks at the cost
of some stamina. If block is performed right before the attack, it is considered a parry
and will deplete enemy's stamina instead.

- O Key: Left Hook. Punch that targets the enemy's low left side.

- P Key: Right Hook. Punch that targets the enemy's low right side.

- W + O Key: Left Jab. Punch that targets the enemy's high left side.

- W + P Key: Right Jab. Punch that targets the enemy's high right side.

- Space Bar: Super Punch. Special punch that will hit through an enemy's guard for 
massive damage

## Mechanics:

The goal of the player is to knock out the enemy within three rounds while avoiding getting knocked down themselves. If either boxers are knocked down three times in one round, it is a TKO and they automatically lose. Health is depleted by landing punches, and a boxer will be knocked down when their health bar reaches 0.
